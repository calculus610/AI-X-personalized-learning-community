# 社区行为准则 / Community Code of Conduct

## 中文

AI-X Community 希望建立一个对学习者、教师、开发者和内容创作者都安全、友好且有建设性的社区。

### 我们鼓励

- 对不同背景、经验、语言与学习方式保持尊重；
- 批评具体的想法、代码和证据，而不是贬低提出它们的人；
- 清楚区分已确认事实、合理推断与尚未验证的假设；
- 对新参与者给予足够上下文，接受善意提问；
- 在发现错误时主动更正，并关注解决问题的下一步。

### 不可接受的行为

- 骚扰、威胁、跟踪、歧视性言论或人身攻击；
- 公开他人的身份、联系方式、学习数据、账号、凭据或私密安全报告；
- 将学术、职业或技术分歧转化为恶意对抗；
- 刷屏、恶意推销、故意干扰讨论或浪费维护者与贡献者的时间；
- 其他在专业、教育或公开协作环境中明显不合适的行为。

### 适用范围与执行

本准则适用于项目仓库、Issue、Pull Request、Discussion、活动、群组以及代表本项目的其他场合。

如果你经历或看到违规行为，请通过维护组织的私密联系渠道报告。不要在公开评论中继续传播他人的私密信息。

维护者将根据影响和上下文采取提醒、删除内容、限制参与、临时禁止或永久禁止等措施。所有报告应尽可能保密、一致且公平地处理。

## English

AI-X Community aims to provide a safe, welcoming, and constructive space for learners, educators, developers, and content creators.

### We encourage

- respect for different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and ways of learning;
- critique of specific ideas, code, and evidence rather than the person presenting them;
- clear separation of confirmed facts, supported inferences, and unverified assumptions;
- sufficient context for new participants and good-faith questions;
- correcting mistakes openly and focusing on the next useful step.

### Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory language, or personal attacks;
- exposing another person's identity, contact information, learning data, account, credential, or private security report;
- turning academic, professional, or technical disagreement into hostile confrontation;
- spam, malicious promotion, deliberate disruption, or intentional waste of contributor time;
- other conduct clearly inappropriate in a professional, educational, or public collaborative setting.

### Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, events, groups, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

Report violations through a private contact channel of the maintaining organization. Do not repeat another person's private information in a public comment.

Maintainers may respond with a reminder, content removal, participation restriction, temporary ban, or permanent ban according to context and impact. Reports should be handled as confidentially, consistently, and fairly as possible.
